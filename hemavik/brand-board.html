01 · The name

What Hemavik means

The name comes from the Sanskrit root hema, gold. It carries three ideas, and every brand decision on this board traces back to one of them.

हेम

Made of gold

Work that holds its value. We build systems that keep paying back long after launch. Kept as a thin thread of Hema gold: the 999 stamp and the "Golden growth" line.

999

Purity

Fine gold is hallmarked 999: nothing extra. Clean automations, clean data, no clutter. Expressed through Kailash snow white and generous space.

शिव

Lord Shiva

Stillness behind constant motion. The crescent and third eye come from Shiva, and the palette comes from Kailash, his mountain home: snow, glacier ice and slate shadow.

02 · Logo

The temple gate mark

An H built as two pillars of a temple gateway, held inside a coin, a nod to hema. Shiva's crescent rests above the crossbar and the bindu, the third eye, sits below it.

  1. Crescent (chandra). Shiva's moon. Calm intelligence at the top of every system.
  2. Pillars. The two sides of the business we join: marketing that brings leads in, operations that serve them.
  3. Bindu. The third eye. Insight: reporting, scoring and AI that see what people miss.
  4. Coin. The outer ring. Value that compounds. Keep clear space of half the mark's width around it.
Minimum size24px on screen, 10mm in print for the mark. 120px wide for the full lockup.
Approved coloursNeelkanth blue on snow, glacier on slate, slate on glacier. Nothing else.
NeverStretch it, add gradients or shadows, rotate it, or place it on busy photos.

05 · Voice

How Hemavik sounds

Like a calm senior consultant: certain, plain-spoken, never hyped.

Pure

Short sentences. One idea at a time. Cut every word that does not carry weight.

Precious

Talk about value the client keeps: hours saved, leads answered, revenue tracked.

Still

Confident without shouting. No exclamation marks, no "revolutionary", no fake urgency.

Say

  • Every new lead gets a WhatsApp reply in under a minute.
  • We build your pipeline once and document it.
  • You own the workflows. We keep them running.

Avoid

  • 10X your business with AI magic!!!
  • Leverage synergistic omnichannel solutions.
  • Limited slots, book now before it's too late.
