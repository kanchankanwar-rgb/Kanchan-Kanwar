FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a GoHighLevel account already?

No. We can set up a new account for you, or work inside your existing one. If you are an agency, we can build snapshots you can resell to your own clients.

What is n8n and why do you use it?

n8n is a workflow automation tool, similar to Zapier or Make, that can be self-hosted. It handles complex logic and AI steps, and costs far less at high volume. We use it wherever GoHighLevel's built-in automations are not enough.

Who owns the workflows and accounts?

You do. Everything is built in your accounts, and you get documentation for every workflow at handover.

How long does a typical project take?

A focused CRM setup takes about two to three weeks. A full system with automations and campaigns usually takes four to six weeks. The blueprint gives you an exact timeline.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses in any time zone.
