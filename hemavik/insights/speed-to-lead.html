Speed to lead: why the first five minutes decide the sale

A lead is most interested at the moment they reach out. Here is how to respond before that interest fades.

By Hemavik Team · 17 Oct 2026

When someone fills in a form or calls your business, they are at the peak of their interest. They have a problem and they are actively looking for someone to solve it. Very often they are contacting your competitors at the same time.

The business that replies first usually wins the conversation. Yet many teams reply hours later, or the next day, because leads arrive while everyone is busy, after hours or on weekends.

Speed to lead is not about working harder. It is about building a system that replies for you. An instant SMS or WhatsApp message confirms the enquiry and asks one useful question. An AI agent can answer common questions and offer a booking link. A task is created for a salesperson with the lead's details and source.

Measure it. Track the time from enquiry to first reply, and from enquiry to booked call. Once replies are automatic, the first number drops to seconds, and your team can spend its time on the conversations that are ready to move forward.
