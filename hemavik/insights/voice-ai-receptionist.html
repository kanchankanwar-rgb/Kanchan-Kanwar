Voice AI receptionists: what they do well, and where people still matter

AI voice agents can answer every call. The best results come from giving them a clear, limited job.

By Hemavik Team · 17 Oct 2026

Voice AI has improved quickly. Modern agents speak naturally, understand interruptions and can look up information while the caller waits. For many businesses, they now handle the calls that used to go to voicemail.

They do well with repetitive, well-defined calls: opening hours, service questions, pricing ranges, booking and rescheduling appointments, and capturing details for a callback. They never get tired, and they answer at 2 a.m. the same way they answer at 2 p.m.

People still matter for complex, emotional or high-value conversations: a complaint, a large purchase or a sensitive medical question. A good setup recognises these moments and transfers the call to a person, with a summary so the caller does not have to repeat themselves.

Start with one clear job, such as after-hours answering and booking. Review transcripts weekly, improve the answers, then expand. Be open with callers that they are speaking to an AI assistant; honesty builds trust.
