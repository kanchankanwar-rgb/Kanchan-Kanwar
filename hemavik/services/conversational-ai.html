Overview

Customers message you on many channels and expect a fast reply. Our AI chat agents answer questions from your knowledge base, capture contact details, book appointments and hand over to your team in the same inbox when a person is needed.

What we deliver

Inside Conversational AI & Chat

Instagram & Facebook DMs

Knowledge base training

Trained on your services, prices, policies and FAQs.

Human handover

Booking in chat

FAQ

Common questions

Will the AI make things up?

We limit the agent to your approved knowledge base and set it to hand over when it is unsure.

Can we see what the AI says?

Yes. Every conversation is visible in your inbox, and you can step in at any time.

Which languages does it support?

Most major languages, including Hindi and Hinglish.
