Overview

Most businesses run on a mix of tools that do not talk to each other. We connect your CRM, website, payment gateway, accounting software, spreadsheets, messaging apps and industry tools through APIs and webhooks, so information is entered once and appears everywhere it is needed.

What we deliver

Inside Third-Party Integrations

App-to-app integrations

Two-way sync between your CRM and the tools your team uses every day.

Custom API connections

Integrations with any tool that offers an API, including Indian and industry-specific software.

Webhooks & real-time triggers

An event in one app instantly starts the right action in another.

Data mapping & clean-up

Fields matched correctly, duplicates prevented and formats standardised.

Payment & accounting sync

Payments, invoices and customers kept in step with your accounting software.

Hosting on your server

Integrations can run on your own server for full control of your data.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you connect a tool that is not on your list?

Most likely. If the tool has an API or supports webhooks, we can connect it.

Who maintains the integrations?

We do. Every integration is monitored, and we update it when an app changes its API.

Is our data secure?

Connections use each app's official API with secure keys, and integrations can run on your own server.
