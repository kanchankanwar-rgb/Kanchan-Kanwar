Overview

A Voice AI agent answers every call in a natural voice. It handles common questions using your business information, captures caller details in the CRM and books appointments straight into your calendar. Complex calls go to your team.

What we deliver

Inside Voice AI Agents

Inbound AI receptionist

Answers every call, day and night, including busy periods and holidays.

Outbound calling

Reminder, follow-up and reactivation calls to contacts who have given consent.

Booking on the call

Checks live availability and books the appointment while the caller is on the line.

Transfer rules

Urgent or complex calls are transferred to the right person with context.

Transcripts & summaries

Every call is recorded, transcribed and summarised in the CRM.

Multilingual agents

Agents that speak English, Hindi and other languages.

FAQ

Common questions

Will callers know they are speaking to AI?

We recommend the agent introduces itself as an AI assistant. Callers respond well when it is honest and helpful.

Can it speak Hindi?

Yes. Agents support several languages, including Hindi. We test quality for your use case before launch.

Can we keep our phone number?

Yes. You can forward your existing number to the agent or port it.
