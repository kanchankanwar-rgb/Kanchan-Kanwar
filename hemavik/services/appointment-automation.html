Customer Engagement

Appointments & Booking

Online booking, reminders and no-show recovery for every calendar in your team.

Talk to an expertWhat we deliver

Overview

Let customers book themselves at any hour, from your website, ads, social profiles or a chat. Confirmations, reminders and reschedule links go out automatically, and missed appointments trigger a follow-up instead of a lost customer.

What we deliver

Inside Appointments & Booking

Booking calendars

Individual, team round-robin, group and class calendars with your availability rules.

Booking everywhere

Booking widgets for your website, landing pages, social profiles and chats.

Reminders

Confirmation and reminder messages by SMS, WhatsApp and email.

No-show recovery

Automatic follow-up and rebooking links when someone misses an appointment.

Calendar sync

Two-way sync with Google and Outlook calendars.

Deposits & pre-payment

Collect a deposit or full payment at the time of booking.

How it works

From first call to running system

  1. 01

    Rules

  2. 02

  3. 03

  4. 04

    Review
