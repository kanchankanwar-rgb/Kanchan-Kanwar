Overview

We set up a CRM around how your team actually sells: pipelines that match your stages, one inbox for calls, SMS, WhatsApp, email and social messages, and automations that move deals forward when people forget.

What we deliver

Inside CRM & Sales Automation

Pipeline design

Deal stages, custom fields and views that match your sales process.

Unified inbox

SMS, WhatsApp, email, Facebook, Instagram and web chat in one conversation view.

Lead capture

Forms, ads, calls and chats flow straight into the CRM with their source.

Lead scoring & assignment

Hot leads reach the right salesperson first, by rules or round-robin.

Follow-up automation

Tasks, reminders and messages triggered by stage changes and inactivity.

Data migration

Contacts, deals and notes moved from spreadsheets or your old CRM.
