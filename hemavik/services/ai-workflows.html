AI-Powered Workflows

Automations that connect your tools and use AI to read, decide and act.

Talk to an expertWhat we deliver

Overview

We find the repetitive work in your business and automate it. Workflows connect your CRM, forms, spreadsheets, accounting and messaging tools, with AI steps that summarise, classify, extract data and draft replies.

What we deliver

Inside AI-Powered Workflows

Automation roadmap

We list your manual processes and rank them by hours saved and effort.

Tool integrations

Connect any tool with an API or webhook: CRM, sheets, accounting, Slack, WhatsApp.

Document processing

AI reads invoices, forms and emails and enters the data where it belongs.

Lead qualification

AI reviews new leads, scores them and routes them to the right person.

Internal assistants

AI assistants trained on your documents and processes to answer staff questions.

Monitoring & care

Error alerts, logs and monthly maintenance so workflows keep running.

Common questions

Is our data safe with AI?

We use business AI APIs whose providers do not train on your data by default, and we can host workflows on your own server.

What happens if a workflow fails?

Every workflow has error handling and alerts. We are notified and fix issues quickly.

Do we need technical staff?

No. We build, document and maintain everything for you.
