About Hemavik

Named for gold. Built for purity.

Hemavik is a digital marketing, automation and AI company. We build connected growth systems for businesses that are ready to stop running on spreadsheets and memory.

The name

What Hemavik means

Hemavik comes from hema (हेम), the Sanskrit word for gold. It stands for three ideas that guide our work.

Made of gold

Systems that hold their value and keep paying back long after launch.

Purity

Like fine gold hallmarked 999: clean automations, clean data, nothing extra.

Lord Shiva

Stillness at the centre of constant motion. Calm, reliable systems behind a busy business.

Our logo is a temple gateway shaped like an H, inside a coin for hema, with Shiva's crescent above and the third eye below. Our colours come from Kailash: snow, glacier ice and slate shadow.

Founder

Kanchan Kanwar

Kanchan Kanwar founded Hemavik to bring marketing, automation and AI together under one roof, so business owners deal with one team instead of five suppliers.

Kanchan leads strategy and delivery on every project, from the first assay call to the systems that keep running long after launch.

I started Hemavik to give growing businesses the kind of connected systems that large companies take for granted: marketing that brings in the right people, automation that follows up every lead, and AI that never misses a call.
