The real problem

Most people don't have a property problem. They have a strategy problem.

Someone can own two, three or even five properties and still have no clear idea how the portfolio is supposed to get them to their financial goal.

The problem normally appears later: borrowing capacity gets tight, cash flow starts hurting, equity is not being used efficiently, or every property is doing exactly the same job.

Rate Your Property Portfolio looks at the fundamentals behind your portfolio and identifies where your strategy is strong and where it may need attention.

A successful property portfolio is not built by simply buying one good property after another. It is built by making sure every property supports the next one.
